# Multi-action trajectory goal-drift representation result

## Decision

Stop the trajectory-connectivity path before acquiring model traces. The
pre-model audit fails its evidence gate for two decisive reasons:

1. Directed goal distance ties spectral connectivity on the favorable topology
   subset. Both separate all three family instances of the same numerical
   sidecar-versus-braided contrast without blocking a legitimate instance.
2. Legitimate and redirected meanings produce identical Rust inputs in the
   complete corpus, so no deterministic score over this frozen graph input can
   distinguish the matched pairs.

No LLM was run and no action was executed. The Rust library remains unchanged,
and no trajectory API is added.

## What was tested

The [frozen protocol](../TRAJECTORY_GOAL_DRIFT_STUDY.md) defines four-action
traces that read a selected primary resource, follow an explicitly requested
linked handle, propose a linked side effect, and propose the primary effect.
The linked side effect is requested in the legitimate partner. In the
redirected partner, the trusted task requests only the primary effect and the
linked result asks for the side effect. That third action is the first harmful
proposal.

The host graph records proposed actions, consumed and produced states, resource
versions, broad grants, declared sinks, and authority anchors. It excludes the
trusted-task and linked-result text and the generator-derived oracle label.
Every matched meaning pair has the same mechanical trace. The `sidecar` motif
keeps the linked proposal on linked-branch states; `braided` also connects it to
the primary result.

The formal corpus contains 12 `mechanism_fit` and 12 `mechanism_check` traces
across message, file, and service families. Each trace contributes four prefix
graphs. `policy.json` was written after fit observation and before any check
graph was constructed or audited. The observed legitimate-block ceiling was
1%.

## Favorable topology subset

The favorable subset keeps legitimate braided traces and redirected sidecar
traces. This deliberately confounds topology with meaning to test whether the
spectral signal separates the two motifs at all. The three family instances on
each side repeat one numerical sidecar-versus-braided contrast; they are not
independent graph cases.

| Signal | Redirected caught | Legitimate blocked |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Negative algebraic connectivity | 3/3 | 0/3 |
| Directed goal distance | 3/3 | 0/3 |
| Authorization gap | 0/3 | 0/3 |
| Off-contract side-effect fraction | 0/3 | 0/3 |
| Weakest goal-support gap | 0/3 | 0/3 |
| Single gateway | 0/3 | 0/3 |
| Natural local cut | 0/3 | 0/3 |
| Node count | 0/3 | 0/3 |

Connectivity reaches 100% recall at zero observed legitimate blocks, but the
linear directed-distance rule reaches the same operating point. The favorable
subset therefore does not show value from the eigensolver.

## Complete collision-aware result

At the first harmful proposal, the complete check has two distinct Rust-input
graphs: one sidecar topology and one braided topology. Each graph occurs under
three legitimate and three redirected cases. The signal vectors collide in the
same way.

| Label-free view | Classes | Cross-label classes | Unavoidable errors | Maximum recall at zero legitimate blocks |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Exact Rust input | 2 | 2 | 6 | 0% |
| Exact mechanical trace | 6 | 6 | 6 | 0% |
| Canonical mechanical trace | 6 | 6 | 6 | 0% |
| Signal vector | 2 | 2 | 6 | 0% |
| Story-augmented mechanics | 12 | 0 | 0 | 100% |

The story-augmented row is a predeclared diagnostic information bound over
synthetic stories constructed with different meanings. Its fingerprint omits
the oracle label, but the story inputs were generated by class. It does not
define, fit, or test a semantic classifier, and the check split repeats the fit
story templates. It only shows that the supplied text contains the distinction
discarded by the graph.

At the 1% fit ceiling, all eight collision-aware thresholds are null. Spectral
connectivity and every deterministic comparison catch 0/6 redirected check
traces and block 0/6 legitimate check traces. With no fitted spectral threshold,
timing classifies all six redirected traces as misses. Its zero late-trigger
count does not test whether some post-harmful-prefix threshold could separate
the motifs.

## Product consequence

Host topology can distinguish the sidecar and braided mechanisms, but those
mechanisms do not determine whether the linked side effect was requested. When
topology and meaning are artificially aligned, directed distance already
captures the distinction. When they are crossed, the graph omits the fact
needed to decide goal alignment.

Do not acquire a trajectory corpus for this representation and do not add a
trajectory gate to the crate. A future hypothesis must mechanically encode a
host-attested distinction between requested and redirected effects, or evaluate
a separately frozen semantic representation of the supplied text. It must also
beat the simplest deterministic rule that consumes the added distinction.
